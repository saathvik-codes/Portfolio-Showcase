# Curated Walkthrough Illustration Prompts — Saathvik Kalepu Portfolio

Every visual beat in this portfolio follows a continuous **conversational walkthrough ("convo")** anchored by a recurring protagonist — a young South Asian software engineer (structured dark charcoal overshirt over cream/black knit tee, rolled sleeves, subtle rimless or thin-frame glasses optional, sharp candid expression) — conversing with domain counterparts tailored to each chapter and real-world engineering use case.

## Art Direction Guardrails (Applied Across All Frames)
- **Visual Medium**: High-craft editorial architectural film illustration / painterly cinema still (tactile grain, architectural lens bokeh, expressive brush & light falloff — zero glossy 3D plastic CGI or generic sci-fi glow).
- **Color Palette**: Obsidian shadow charcoal (`#0b0b0c`), warm tungsten amber practicals (`#eab370`), soft alabaster paper (`#f2ede4`), controlled iris-violet rim reflection (`#9d8cff`).
- **Strictly Forbidden**: Speech bubbles, legible text, gibberish UI words, floating glowing brains, humanoid robots, stock cyberpunk neon grids.
- **Aspect Ratio**: Landscape `16:10` / `16:9` widescreen frames for cinematic scroll stages (`public/illustrations/*.jpg`).
