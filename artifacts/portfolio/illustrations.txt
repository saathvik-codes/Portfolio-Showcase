ILLUSTRATIONS — journey (scrapbook) design
==========================================

This branch has its OWN set, separate from the walkthrough branch.
Put files in:  artifacts/portfolio/public/illustrations/journey/
Use the exact filenames below. Until a file exists the slot shows a plain
paper placeholder, so you can add them one at a time.

11 files. Most sit inside taped polaroids, so compose for the crop noted.


THE MEDIUM (paste before every prompt)
--------------------------------------
Cut-paper collage, made by hand: shapes cut from coloured craft paper and
kraft card with visible scissor edges, slight paper shadows where layers
overlap, a few torn edges, and small details added with a black felt-tip
pen. Flat, no gradients, no lighting effects. Photographed straight on,
like a page from a scrapbook.
Paper colours only: terracotta (#b4532a), deep teal (#2f6b66), mustard,
kraft brown, cream, and black pen. Background is plain cream paper.
People are built from simple paper shapes: oval heads, dot eyes, no
mouths or a single pen line, no fingers (mitten hands or hidden hands).
Playful and a little naive, like a picture book made with scissors.
No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no speech bubbles, no 3D, no glow.

Negative prompt (if supported):
photorealistic, 3d, render, digital painting, gradient, glow, bokeh,
detailed face, detailed hands, fingers, text, letters, watermark, vector,
glossy, anime, airbrush

THE CHARACTER (same in every image)
-----------------------------------
Saathvik: young South Asian man, black paper hair cut in a messy wave,
round glasses drawn in pen, teal paper overshirt, cream tee, terracotta
sneakers. Always doing something with another person.

MAKE IT FEEL HANDMADE
---------------------
- Choose the result with the most visible scissor edges and imperfect shapes.
- Add a real paper texture overlay at about 20% Multiply.
- Export JPG, 1400 px on the long edge, under 350 KB.
