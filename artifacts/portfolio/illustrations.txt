WHY THE LAST BATCH LOOKED AI-MADE (avoid all of these)
------------------------------------------------------
- Glossy, evenly-lit "perfect" rendering, airbrushed gradients, soft glow.
- Too much detail everywhere; every surface equally sharp.
- Cinematic lighting, bokeh, film-still realism.
- Detailed faces and hands (the classic giveaway).
- Symmetric composition, centred hero pose, stock-photo smiles.


THE MEDIUM (paste this block before every prompt)
-------------------------------------------------
Hand-drawn sketchbook illustration: loose black fineliner pen lines with
uneven, wobbly line weight, light visible pencil construction lines left in,
and a flat transparent watercolour wash that bleeds past the lines in places.
Drawn on off-white cold-press paper with visible paper tooth and a slightly
yellowed edge. Only three colours of wash: indigo blue, burnt-orange clay,
and warm grey; everything else is bare paper. Lots of empty paper around the
figures; background suggested with a few quick lines, not rendered.
Faces simplified: dot eyes, a single line for the mouth, no detailed features.
Hands simplified or tucked away. Slightly off-kilter, imperfect perspective,
like an architect's travel sketch or a New Yorker spot drawing.
NOT digital painting, NOT 3D, NOT anime, NOT vector, no gradients, no glow,
no bokeh, no text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no speech bubbles.

Negative prompt (if your tool supports one):
photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, glossy, smooth shading, airbrush, gradient,
bokeh, cinematic lighting, detailed face, detailed hands, symmetry, text,
watermark, signature, neon, glow, vector art, anime, plastic


THE CHARACTER (identical in every image)
----------------------------------------
Saathvik: young South Asian man, short messy dark hair drawn in a few strokes,
thin round glasses, charcoal overshirt with rolled sleeves (indigo wash),
cream tee (bare paper). Always mid-conversation with someone, seen at a slight
angle, never posing for the viewer (except in the contact image).


AFTER GENERATING (this is what sells "handmade")
------------------------------------------------
1. Pick the loosest, most imperfect result, not the prettiest.
2. Desaturate about 15% and warm the whites slightly toward cream.
3. Overlay a real paper-texture scan at 20–30% Multiply.
4. Optional but best: print it, trace the key lines yourself with a pen,
   scan back in. Even five minutes of real tracing removes the AI feel.
5. Export JPG, 1600 px long edge, under 400 KB.
Crop guide: site shows 4:5 on desktop and 16:10 on mobile, so keep the
action inside the middle 70% of the frame.
